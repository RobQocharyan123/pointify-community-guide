# Troubleshooting a Pointify points display

Point totals can appear delayed or different when an action is still processing, eligibility conditions were not met, the wrong account is open, or local browser data is stale. Begin with the official site [pointify-token.com](https://pointify-token.com/) or bot [@pointify_official_bot](https://t.me/pointify_official_bot).

## Safe checks

1. Confirm that you are viewing the intended account and official domain.
2. Read the current task conditions, limits, dates, and eligibility notes.
3. Refresh once, then retry in an updated browser or official app route.
4. Record the task name, expected result, actual result, and approximate completion time.
5. If the discrepancy remains, prepare a privacy-safe support request.

Do not pay someone to alter a balance, run scripts from strangers, or share account secrets. Displayed points describe platform participation; they are not money and do not guarantee payment, conversion, token allocation, withdrawal, or future value.
