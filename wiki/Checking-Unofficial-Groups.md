# Checking an unofficial Pointify group

Community groups can be useful, but a group name, logo, member count, or administrator badge does not prove official status. Compare every important claim with [pointify-token.com](https://pointify-token.com/) or [@pointify_official_bot](https://t.me/pointify_official_bot).

## Warning signs

- Administrators privately request passwords, login codes, private keys, or recovery phrases.
- A pinned message uses a look-alike domain or shortened destination that cannot be inspected.
- Members are promised guaranteed profit, a fixed token allocation, or special access after paying a fee.
- Questions are deleted while users are pressured to act immediately.

Treat unofficial discussion as community commentary, not product policy. Verify rules, dates, eligibility, and support instructions at an official entry point before acting.
