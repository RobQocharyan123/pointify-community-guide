# Writing a privacy-safe Pointify support request

A useful support request contains enough technical detail to reproduce a problem without exposing the account owner. Reach support from [pointify-token.com](https://pointify-token.com/) or the official bot [@pointify_official_bot](https://t.me/pointify_official_bot).

## Include

- what you expected and what actually happened;
- the date, time, device type, operating system, browser, and app version;
- the exact error message;
- the smallest sequence of steps that reproduces the problem;
- a screenshot with personal information covered.

## Never include

Do not send a password, login code, private key, recovery phrase, full identity document, or unnecessary financial information. Do not publish an email address, phone number, or account identifier in a public issue.

If somebody claiming to be support asks for a secret or an advance payment, stop and verify the contact through an official entry point.
