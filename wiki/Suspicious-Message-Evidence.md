# Recording a suspicious Pointify message safely

If a message claims to represent Pointify but asks for payment, credentials, or urgent action, do not follow its link. Open the known official entry points independently:

- Website: [pointify-token.com](https://pointify-token.com/)
- Telegram bot: [@pointify_official_bot](https://t.me/pointify_official_bot)

## Evidence worth preserving

Record the complete URL, sender username, date, time, and the exact claim. A screenshot can help, but hide your phone number, email address, balances, login codes, and other personal data before sharing it.

Do not reply with a password, one-time login code, private key, or recovery phrase. If you opened the link, close it, review active sessions, and change any reused password from a trusted device.

## A useful report

A concise report says what was expected, what happened, where it happened, and how someone can reproduce the issue without exposing an account. Send it only through a support route reached from the official site or bot.
