# Responsible Pointify referral disclosure

Referral links should be shared with context, consent, and a clear disclosure. A reader should know before clicking whether the publisher may receive participation credit.

For an independent route, provide the official website [pointify-token.com](https://pointify-token.com/) and official Telegram bot [@pointify_official_bot](https://t.me/pointify_official_bot) alongside any referral link.

## A transparent referral note

A good note explains:

1. that the URL is a referral link;
2. what benefit the referrer may receive;
3. that participation is optional;
4. that points are not money and do not guarantee a payment, conversion, token allocation, or future value.

Avoid countdown pressure, guaranteed-profit language, fabricated endorsements, or posting the same message repeatedly in unrelated communities. Follow each community's promotion rules.
