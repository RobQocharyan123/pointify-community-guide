# Changing devices safely with Pointify

When moving to a new phone or browser, start from [pointify-token.com](https://pointify-token.com/) or [@pointify_official_bot](https://t.me/pointify_official_bot). Do not use a login link sent by an unknown account.

## Before the move

- Confirm that you control the email or Telegram account associated with your access.
- Review active sessions and close sessions you no longer recognize.
- Update a reused or exposed password before signing in on the new device.
- Install operating-system and browser security updates.

## After the move

Check recent account activity and notifications. Remove Pointify data from a device you are selling or giving away only after confirming that the new device works.

Official support should never need your password, one-time code, private key, or recovery phrase. Anyone requesting those secrets should be treated as suspicious.
