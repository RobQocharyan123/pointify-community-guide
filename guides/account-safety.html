Pointify account-safety checklist

Begin from the official Pointify website or open the verified @pointify_official_bot link directly.

Never share these details

  • Your Telegram login code or account password
  • A private key, seed phrase, or wallet recovery phrase
  • Remote-access credentials or screen-sharing control

Common warning signs

  • Guaranteed-profit or guaranteed-token-value promises
  • Pressure to act immediately or bypass official support
  • Lookalike domains, misspelled bot names, and unsolicited direct messages
  • Requests to transfer assets in order to unlock points

Read the official fraud-prevention guide and contact the Help Centre if something looks suspicious.
