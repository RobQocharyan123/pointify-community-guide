How Pointify works

Pointify is a Telegram Mini App experience designed around eligible activities, in-platform participation points, daily codes, invitations, and leaderboard progress.

A typical user journey

  1. Start at the official Pointify website and review the current information.
  2. Open the @pointify_official_bot Telegram Mini App.
  3. Check which activities are currently available and read their requirements.
  4. Complete only eligible actions and follow the recorded progress in the app.
  5. Use the help centre if an activity or account result is unclear.
Important: features, requirements, and eligibility can change. The official product pages are the authoritative source.

Where to get help

Visit the Pointify Help Centre or browse the official guides.
