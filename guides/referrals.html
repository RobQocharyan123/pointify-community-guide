Using Pointify referrals responsibly

Pointify may provide invitation tools that let users share the official Telegram Mini App with friends. Create or copy referral information only through @pointify_official_bot and check the current rules before sharing.

Good sharing practice

  • Describe Pointify accurately and avoid guaranteed-reward claims.
  • Tell recipients that participation is optional.
  • Do not post repeatedly in unrelated groups or private conversations.
  • Never request another person's login code, password, private key, or seed phrase.
Pointify points measure in-platform participation. They are not money and do not guarantee token allocation, payment, conversion, withdrawal, profit, or future value.
