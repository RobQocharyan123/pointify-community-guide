Pointify daily codes

Daily codes are one of the participation tools available through the official Pointify experience. Availability, timing, and eligibility can change, so verify the current instructions inside the @pointify_official_bot.

Safe workflow

  1. Open Pointify from an official link.
  2. Read the active daily-code instructions before entering a code.
  3. Check the result and recorded progress in the app.
  4. Do not pay anyone for a code or disclose account credentials.
Points notice: participation points are not money and do not guarantee a token allocation, payment, withdrawal, profit, or future value.

For current help, visit the official Pointify website and Help Centre.
