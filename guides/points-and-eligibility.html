Pointify points and eligibility

Pointify points are an in-platform measure of participation. Users may receive points for activities that meet the current requirements shown in the Telegram Mini App.

Points are not money. They do not guarantee token allocation, payment, conversion, withdrawal, profit, or future value.

Before completing an activity

  • Read the current activity description and eligibility requirements.
  • Confirm that you are using the official @pointify_official_bot.
  • Do not rely on screenshots, copied messages, or third-party profit claims.
  • Check recorded progress inside the official product experience.
