Understanding Pointify leaderboards

Leaderboards help users compare recorded participation progress inside Pointify. Positions can change as eligible activity is processed and other users participate.

How to interpret a ranking

  • Treat it as a snapshot of in-platform activity, not as an account balance.
  • Check the official app for the current value and position.
  • Review eligibility requirements if expected activity is not reflected.
  • Use official support rather than sharing private account information publicly.
No financial guarantee: points and leaderboard positions are not money and do not guarantee tokens, payments, withdrawals, profit, or future value.
