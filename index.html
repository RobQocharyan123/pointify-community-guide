What users can do

Pointify users can complete eligible activities, collect in-platform points, use daily codes, invite friends, and follow leaderboard progress. Check the current requirements on the official site before participating.

Points notice

Pointify points measure participation inside the platform. They are not money and do not guarantee token allocation, payment, conversion, withdrawal, profit, or future value.

Stay safe

  1. Use only official Pointify links.
  2. Never share passwords or Telegram login codes.
  3. Never share private keys, seed phrases, or recovery phrases.
  4. Be cautious of guaranteed-profit or guaranteed-value claims.
