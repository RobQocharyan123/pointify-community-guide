# Pointify community guide security policy

## Reporting a suspicious link or account

If you encounter a website, Telegram account, or message that appears to impersonate Pointify, do not provide credentials or interact with payment requests.

Compare the destination with the official resources:

- Website: [pointify-token.com](https://pointify-token.com/)
- Telegram Mini App: [@pointify_official_bot](https://t.me/pointify_official_bot)
- Help Centre: [pointify-token.com/help](https://pointify-token.com/help)
- Fraud-prevention guide: [pointify-token.com/guides/fraud-prevention](https://pointify-token.com/guides/fraud-prevention)

For product or account help, use the official Help Centre. For a security concern in this public documentation repository, open a private security advisory through the repository's **Security** tab.

## Sensitive information

Pointify support should never need:

- your password or Telegram login code;
- a private key, seed phrase, or recovery phrase;
- remote access to your device;
- a payment made to unlock participation points.

Do not publish sensitive account details in a GitHub issue or discussion.

## Financial-claims notice

Pointify points are an in-platform participation measure. They are not money and do not guarantee token allocation, payment, conversion, withdrawal, profit, or future value.
